# TeleTree Bros — Estimate Tool Variables

These are the factors the system uses to generate each estimate. All values are adjustable — review and update as needed based on your experience.

---

## Base Pricing by Tree Height

| Tree Size       | Height       | Base Range         |
|-----------------|--------------|--------------------|
| Small           | Under 30 ft  | $3,000 – $4,000    |
| Medium          | 30 – 60 ft   | $3,500 – $6,000    |
| Large           | 60 – 80 ft   | $5,000 – $9,000    |
| Very Large      | 80 ft+       | $7,000 – $15,000+  |

**Minimum estimate: $3,000** (no job quoted below this)

---

## Adjustment Factors

Each factor increases the base estimate by a percentage. Multiple factors can stack.

| Factor                          | Adjustment     | Notes                                                        |
|---------------------------------|----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Proximity to Structure**      | +15% – 40%    | House, garage, shed, or other building within 20 ft          |
| **Power Line Proximity**        | +15% – 30%    | Overhead lines within the canopy or drop zone                |
| **Limited Access**              | +10% – 25%    | No clear path for equipment; tight lot, steep grade, etc.    |
| **Hardwood Species**            | +10% – 20%    | Oak, hickory, maple — denser wood = more labor and equipment time |
| **Dead / Hazardous Tree**       | +10% – 15%    | Unpredictable breakage, additional safety precautions        |
| **Emergency / Urgent**          | +25% – 50%    | Customer flags the job as emergency or dangerous             |

---

## Factors Shown to the Customer

The estimate result displays these as "Factors Affecting Your Estimate" with impact levels:

| Impact Level | Label         | Displayed When                                  |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| High         | High Impact   | Proximity to structure, very large tree size     |
| Medium       | Medium Impact | Power lines, cleanup complexity, tree condition  |
| Low          | Low Impact    | Good access, softwood species, small cleanup     |

---

## Difficulty Rating

Each estimate includes a difficulty badge visible to the customer:

| Rating           | Criteria                                                              |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Standard**     | Open area, good access, no nearby structures or lines                 |
| **Complex**      | Near structure OR power lines, limited access, hardwood, or dead tree |
| **High-Complexity** | Multiple complicating factors — structure + lines + limited access |

---

## Detail Cards Shown to Customer

The estimate result displays four summary cards:

1. **Species** — Identified from the photo (e.g., "Red Oak — Hardwood, dense canopy")
2. **Estimated Height** — Approximated from photo analysis (e.g., "60–70 ft")
3. **Estimated Time** — Crew hours based on size and complexity (e.g., "6–8 hours")
4. **Difficulty** — Standard / Complex / High-Complexity with a short explanation

---

## What the Customer Sees vs. Doesn't See

| Visible to Customer                  | Internal Only (not shown)              |
|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Price range                          | Exact percentage adjustments           |
| Difficulty rating                    | Base price before adjustments          |
| Factor names + impact level          | Individual factor dollar amounts       |
| Species, height, time estimates      | Pricing formula / variable weights     |
| "Subject to on-site evaluation" note | —                                      |
