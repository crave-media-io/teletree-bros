# TeleTree Bros — Estimate Tool Variables

These are the factors the system uses to generate each estimate. All values are adjustable — review and update as needed based on your experience.

Pricing is calibrated against national market data (Angi, Lawn Love, HomeAdvisor) and positioned at the upper end of standard rates. The Merlo Roto + Woodcracker CS750 equipment context is factored into the analysis — proximity and access risks are rated lower than traditional crew estimates since the grapple system eliminates free-fall.

---

## Base Pricing by Tree Height

| Tree Size       | Height       | Base Range       |
|-----------------|--------------|------------------|
| Small           | Under 30 ft  | $300 – $800      |
| Medium          | 30 – 60 ft   | $800 – $2,000    |
| Large           | 60 – 80 ft   | $1,200 – $2,500  |
| Very Large      | 80 ft+       | $1,800 – $4,000  |

**Minimum estimate:** $500 (no job quoted below this)
**Structural contact minimum:** $2,000 (tree on/against a building)

## Adjustment Factors

Adjustments are **additive** — all applicable percentages are summed and applied once to the base price. This prevents compounding.

| Factor                          | Adjustment     | Notes                                                        |
|---------------------------------|----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Proximity to Structure**      | +10% – 20%    | House, garage, shed within 20 ft. Grapple system reduces risk vs. traditional crews |
| **Power Line Proximity**        | +20% – 35%    | Overhead lines in the work zone. May require utility coordination or line disconnect |
| **Limited Access**              | +5% – 15%     | No clear path for equipment; tight lot, steep grade. Rated based on crane reachability from street/driveway |
| **Hardwood Species**            | +5% – 10%     | Oak, hickory, maple — denser wood = heavier sections and longer crane time |
| **Declining Tree**              | +5% – 10%     | Partial dieback, disease, structural compromise — adds unpredictability |
| **Dead / Hazardous Tree**       | +10% – 20%    | Fully dead or structurally compromised — brittle wood, unpredictable breakage |
| **Tree On Structure**           | +30% – 50%    | Tree has fallen on or is leaning against a building. Careful sequencing required to avoid further damage |
| **Emergency / Urgent**          | +15% – 30%    | Customer-flagged OR auto-detected by the system when structural contact or imminent fall risk is identified |

**Maximum adjustment cap:** 75% for standard jobs, 125% for structural contact jobs

---

## Auto-Emergency Detection

The system can flag a job as emergency even if the customer doesn't check the emergency box. This triggers when:
- A tree is detected resting on or against a structure
- A tree is in imminent danger of falling on a structure

This ensures emergency-level pricing applies to genuinely urgent situations regardless of how the customer self-reports.

## Factors Shown to the Customer

The estimate result displays these as "Factors Affecting Your Estimate" with impact levels:

| Impact Level | Label         | Displayed When                                  |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| High         | High Impact   | Structural contact, power lines, large dead tree |
| Medium       | Medium Impact | Proximity, cleanup complexity, tree condition    |
| Low          | Low Impact    | Good access, softwood species, small cleanup     |

---

## Difficulty Rating

Each estimate includes a difficulty badge visible to the customer:

| Rating              | Criteria                                                              |
|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Standard**        | Open area, good access, no nearby structures or lines                 |
| **Complex**         | Near structure OR power lines, limited access, hardwood, or dead tree |
| **High-Complexity** | Multiple complicating factors — structure + lines + limited access, or tree on structure |

---

## Detail Cards Shown to Customer

The estimate result displays four summary cards:

1. **Wood Type** — Hardwood or Softwood with density note (light/moderate/heavy). Potential species shown as a suggestion below, not a definitive ID.
2. **Estimated Height** — Approximated from photo analysis using reference objects (houses, vehicles, fences, etc.)
3. **Estimated Time** — Crew hours based on size and complexity, calibrated for crane/grapple operations
4. **Condition** — Healthy / Declining / Dead / Hazardous

## What the Customer Sees vs. Doesn't See

| Visible to Customer                  | Internal Only (not shown)              |
|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|

| Factor names + impact level          | Individual factor dollar amounts       |
| Wood type, height, time estimates    | Pricing formula / variable weights     |
| "Subject to on-site evaluation" note | Auto-emergency detection logic         |
